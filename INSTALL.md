# How to install Calm Path

Pick your device and follow the steps. Calm Path is free, needs no account, and keeps everything on
your own device.

---

## 📱 Android phone (recommended: the full app with reminders)

1. On your phone, open this page:
   **https://github.com/tarunabhdutta/calm-path/releases/latest**
2. Under **Assets**, tap **`CalmPath-<version>.apk`** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file, from the notification or from the **Files / Downloads** app.
4. Android asks whether to allow installing apps from this source (Chrome or Files). Tap
   **Settings → allow**, then go back.
5. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details → Install
   anyway**. The app isn't on the Play Store, which is why the warning appears.
6. Open **Calm Path**, type your name, and tap **Start my island**.
7. When asked, **allow notifications** so the gentle daily reminders work.

**Updating later:** download the newer APK and open it. It installs over the old one and **keeps
your data**. Don't uninstall first, because uninstalling deletes the data on the phone.

---

## 🍎 iPhone or iPad (web app)

1. Open **Safari** and go to **https://tarunabhdutta.github.io/calm-path/**
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open Calm Path from your home screen. It runs full-screen and works offline.

*Daily reminders aren't available in the iPhone web version. You can set an iPhone reminder or a
Shortcuts automation that opens the app at the same times.*

---

## 🪟 Windows PC

1. Go to **https://github.com/tarunabhdutta/calm-path/releases/latest**
2. Under **Assets**, download **`Calm-Path-Setup-<version>.exe`**.
3. Double-click it. It installs in one click, adds a desktop shortcut and opens the app.
4. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**. The app
   isn't code-signed, which is why the warning appears.

Your data is kept in `%APPDATA%\Calm Path`, and the app makes a daily backup to
`Documents\Calm Path Backups`. You can change the backup folder in Settings.

---

## 💻 Mac, Linux, or any computer (web app)

Open **https://tarunabhdutta.github.io/calm-path/** in Chrome or Edge, then click the **install**
icon in the address bar. Or simply bookmark the page. It works offline after the first visit.

---

## Moving to a new phone or computer

Each device keeps its own data. To move it:
1. On the old device: **Settings → Back up now.** On Android the file goes to the Documents
   folder; on the web it downloads.
2. Copy the `.calmpath` file to the new device.
3. On the new device: **Settings → Restore a backup**, and choose the file.
